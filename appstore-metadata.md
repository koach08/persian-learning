## Description (4000 chars max, English)
Persian Learning is a comprehensive app for learning Persian (Farsi), designed specifically for Japanese speakers. Whether you want to connect with family, travel to Iran, or explore Persian literature, this app takes you from zero to fluent.

STRUCTURED LESSONS — A1 TO C2
Over 100 guided lessons across all six CEFR levels. Each lesson introduces phrases with audio, romanization, and Japanese translation. You listen, repeat, and practice through varied step types — dictation, word reordering, multiple choice, and cloze exercises. Lessons unlock progressively so you always have a clear path forward.

4,626 VOCABULARY WORDS
A carefully organized vocabulary database covering everyday topics (food, family, weather, body parts) through advanced domains (economics, philosophy, literature, law). Every word includes Persian script, romanization, Japanese meaning, and CEFR level tagging. Learn them through SRS flashcards that schedule reviews at optimal intervals.

AI CONVERSATION PRACTICE
Talk with an AI partner across 24 real-life scenarios — ordering at a cafe, visiting a doctor, debating social topics, discussing poetry. Four scenarios per CEFR level. The AI speaks Persian, provides suggested replies, and gives a detailed review of your grammar and vocabulary after each session.

PRONUNCIATION SCORING
Record your voice and get word-by-word accuracy, fluency, and completeness scores powered by GPT-4o audio analysis. Persian is one of the few languages where standard speech APIs fall short — this app uses a custom approach to give you meaningful pronunciation feedback.

SPACED REPETITION (SRS)
Flashcards with a built-in spaced repetition algorithm. Cards you struggle with appear more often. Cards you know well fade into longer intervals. Track your mastery rate and due reviews from the home screen.

EXERCISES & DRILLS
Four exercise types to reinforce what you learn:
- Listening quiz: hear a word or phrase, pick the correct meaning
- Dictation: hear Persian, type what you hear in Persian script
- Cloze (fill-in-the-blank): complete sentences with missing words
- Sentence building: arrange shuffled words into correct order

SHADOWING & LISTENING
Practice natural rhythm and intonation by shadowing native-speed audio. Passive listening mode lets you absorb Persian while doing other things.

VERB CONJUGATION
75 essential verbs with full conjugation tables — present, past, and progressive forms for all six persons. Drill conjugations interactively.

READING COMPREHENSION
AI-generated reading passages matched to your CEFR level, with vocabulary lists, comprehension questions, and full translations.

MINIMAL PAIRS
Train your ear to distinguish similar-sounding Persian sounds that Japanese speakers often confuse.

GAMIFICATION
Earn XP for every activity. Maintain a daily streak. Set daily study goals. Watch your level grow on the progress dashboard.

DAILY STUDY FLOW
One tap to start your daily session: SRS review, next lesson, then conversation practice — or choose the 5-minute micro session for busy days.

MY WORDS
Save any word to your personal list during lessons and conversations. Review them anytime from the My Words section.

【AI会話練習】
AIパートナーと24のリアルなシーン（カフェでの注文、病院での会話、社会問題の議論、詩の話など）で練習できます。各CEFRレベルに4つのシナリオ。返答のヒントも出るので、初心者でも安心です。終了後には文法や語彙の振り返りレビューがもらえます。

覚えにくい単語は短い間隔で、得意な単語は長い間隔で復習。ホーム画面から復習待ちの件数がすぐ分かります。

【シャドーイング & 聞き流し】
ネイティブの速度でリズムとイントネーションを練習。聞き流しモードでは、ながら学習もできます。

【動詞活用表】

【読解練習】

【マイ単語帳】

## Privacy Policy
Note: A privacy policy URL is required before submission.

Data handling summary:
- Audio recordings are sent to the app's server-side API for pronunciation assessment (processed via OpenAI GPT-4o audio). Audio is not stored after processing.
- Conversation text is sent to OpenAI API for AI partner responses. No user data is retained by OpenAI per their API data usage policy.
- Reading passages and some exercises are generated via OpenAI API.
- All learning progress (SRS data, XP, streak, lesson completion) is stored locally on the device only. No account or cloud sync.
- No personal information is collected. No analytics SDK is included.
